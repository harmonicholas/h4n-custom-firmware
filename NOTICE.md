# Scope and attribution

The MIT license covers original contributions to this project, including tools and documentation. It does not grant rights to Zoom's firmware, trademarks, manuals or any other third-party material. A reconstructed firmware image contains proprietary upstream material and is not made MIT-licensed by this project.

This is an independent community project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Zoom. H4n and Zoom are used to identify the compatible product.

No complete firmware image, bootloader dump, manufacturer manual, personal recording or private development session is included. Users supply their own exact original image. The patch is a sparse XOR delta, not an encryption or access-control mechanism.

Developed through hardware testing by the project maintainer with AI-assisted reverse engineering, coding and analysis using OpenAI Codex. Bounded models are not full emulators; hardware evidence and limitations are documented separately.
